Vision

New city-states, where people live under self-chosen rules.

Communities governing themselves, defining customs and laws that reflect their unique identity.

Different people, with different lifestyles, cultures and laws.

Each community thrives with its own traditions, practices, and legal frameworks.

Reverse diaspora: self-governing communities, instead of one-size-fits-all states.

Rather than a single, uniform state, people form tailored communities based on shared values and visions.

                                                                                                                                                                                               

A network of city-states, based on common values and mutual benefit.

People living meaningful lives, mentally healthy and empowered with true agency.

Foundational Infrastructure Pieces

This vision requires multiple foundational infrastructure pieces.
One of those being culture creation and -management, such that each city-state can construct and sustain its own new culture on a state level.

This infrastructure piece is CultureForge.
